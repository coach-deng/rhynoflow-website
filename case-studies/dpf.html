The situation

Dansk Puslespilsforening is a small, entirely volunteer-run forening with a 4-person board. They organise Denmark's national puzzle championship, manage 100+ members, coordinate sponsors, and handle all the logistics of a serious competitive event — all while holding down day jobs.

The inbox was the bottleneck. Every membership inquiry, sponsorship email, and event question landed in one shared Gmail account. The board took turns drafting replies, chasing each other on WhatsApp, and trying to remember who owned which task from the last board meeting.

What Rhynoflow built

We deployed a multiagent stack built specifically for DPF's workflows:

  • An email drafter that reads every incoming message and writes a reply in the board's voice — warm, direct, Danish — and drops it in Gmail drafts for one-click review.
  • A skip-list agent that filters out MobilePay notifications, hosting emails, and other auto-senders that never need a response.
  • A meeting notes agent that reads the board meeting Google Doc and extracts every action item, assignee, and deadline into a colour-coded task sheet — Maiken in pink, Sofie in red, Marianne in orange, Olivia in green.
  • An inbox cleaner that archives threads automatically once the board has replied.
  • A weekly Monday briefing that lands at 07:00 with the week's calendar, open tasks, and anything that needs attention — no digging required.

The result

Within two weeks of going live, the board stopped spending Sunday evenings catching up on emails. The task sheet from their May board meeting had 48 action items extracted automatically — colour-coded, assigned, and ready. The inbox now drafts itself.

  • ~5 hours saved per week across the board
  • 48 tasks auto-extracted from one meeting doc
  • Zero inbox backlog within the first week
  • Weekly briefing replaces the "what did we agree on?" WhatsApp thread
